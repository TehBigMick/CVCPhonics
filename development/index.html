What to do next

If you are concerned, if the child has lost a skill, or if something does not feel right, share this summary with the child’s health professional. Ask about a validated developmental screening or referral rather than waiting for the next check-in.

How it was created

Original questions, informed by public milestones.

01

Observe, don’t perform

Use recent examples from ordinary routines. A child does not need to demonstrate every skill on demand.

02

Consider the whole child

Culture, home languages, opportunity, health and context can all affect how a skill is shown.

03

Act on concerns

This tool supports conversation. Only validated tools administered appropriately can provide developmental screening.

Content model informed by the CDC developmental milestones and American Academy of Pediatrics guidance. The five-area layout and “Yes”, “Sometimes” and “Not yet” choices provide a familiar format, but every observation is independently written and no licensed ASQ-3 questions, scoring thresholds or interpretations are used. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASQ-3, the CDC or the AAP.
