4 Try it together

Short, playful practice away from the screen.

Point and say

Ask “Where is your head?” Your child points and says “This is my head.” Practise “These are my hands” and “I have two feet”.

Make a funny body

Draw a friendly monster with two heads or three arms. Count together and say “It has three arms.” Keep the drawing simple and let your child choose.

Use our little words

Practise my, I and can in real sentences: “This is my head”, “I have two hands” and “I can clap”. Do the action as you say it.

Move, then freeze!

Stand up together. Try the action, then freeze until the next one. Take turns giving the instructions.
