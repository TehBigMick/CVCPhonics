4 Try it together

Short, playful practice away from the screen.

Colour hunt

Ask “Show me something red.” Find one object of each colour, then say “It’s red.” Change the order and let your child give you a colour to find.

Make a pattern

Use coloured blocks: red, blue, red… Ask “What comes next?” Start with two colours. Make a new pattern together.

Cc in our world

Find a cup or a toy car. Say the word slowly, listen for /k/ and draw a big C and a small c in the air.

In class this week: colour vocabulary, “It’s…” sentences, Paint with Me, The Painters and a playful review.

Five to ten minutes is plenty. Pointing or copying an action is a good first step; help your child try a word or short sentence when they are ready.
